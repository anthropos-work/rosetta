## `D-M257x-233-2` — a behind-count is graded against a ROLE, and nothing records one

`stack-demo/rosetta-extensions` is **159 commits behind** `origin/main`. Reported as a health finding that
looks worse than `app`'s 28. It is the opposite: per-stack rext copies are **pinned-tag consumption
copies** by design (`CLAUDE.md`, the two-clone-role model), so 159 is the contract working. `app`'s 28,
on a clone that is meant to track, is the one that matters.

The census cannot tell them apart, and neither can anything else on disk — **the number is identical; the
meaning is inverted.** Rather than hard-code a role table (a hand-maintained tuple, `§2`'s recurring
defect), this is recorded as `ROUTE-M257x-233-behind-count-has-no-ROLE`: the role a clone plays is already
declared in `clones.pin.json`, which `clone_pin_guard` reads, and a health view that ignored it is the
guard-family's data being derived twice.
