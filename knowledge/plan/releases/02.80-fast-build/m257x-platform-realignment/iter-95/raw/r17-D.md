  (`cms` is handled correctly by contrast — `service_taxonomy.md:131` says *"repo frozen, **not** archived"*,
  and `cms` indeed has commits on 2026-08-04.)
- **Confidence:** medium-high. I hesitated because a repo can be un-archived, written to, and re-archived,
