## What belongs here

- **Every re-scope-trigger ruling.** A clause that cannot be met whose fix belongs to a **closed**
  milestone goes to the user with three options — **re-open**, **carry with a NAMED destination**, or
  **drop** — and the answer is recorded here, with who decided and on what basis. A carry to "the next
  release" or "a future pass" is not a destination.
- **Every denominator pin** that changes how a clause is graded — the annotation-request count
  (eight / ten labels / seven reviewer items), the demo-patch inventory, the Playthrough count.
- **Every substitute instrument** adopted because the documented one does not exist on a path (e.g. the
  dev-side equivalent of the batch gate, or of `up-injected.sh`'s READY definition).
- **Any ruling that a clause is discharged in a form other than the one written** — including whether a
  voice **NO-GO** discharges the B2 voice clause.
- **Any decision to close the release with a clause RED.** That is a user call, never a checkpoint call,
  and it is not recorded anywhere else.

**What does NOT belong here:** the findings themselves (they go to
[`spec-notes.md`](spec-notes.md)) and their routing (that goes to
[`progress.md`](progress.md) § Findings routed out of this milestone). A decision is the *ruling*, not the
observation that prompted it.
