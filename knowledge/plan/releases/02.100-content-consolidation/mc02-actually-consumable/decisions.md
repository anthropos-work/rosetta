## What belongs in this file

MC02 is a **checkpoint**. Its characteristic decision is **a routing**, not a design choice:

- **Every clause graded RED gets a decision here** — which milestone it goes back to (M267 / M269 / M270),
  on what evidence, and what the receiving milestone has to change. **A routing is not a routing until the
  TARGET's own doc says so** — the failure that fired ≥3× in v2.8. Record the routing here *and* land it
  in the target's `overview.md`.
- **Any stated exception to a clause** — e.g. if clause 4 proves demo-only by construction — is a decision
  here, argued in writing. A clause quietly graded once instead of twice is not an exception; it is a
  defect.
- **Any measurement-shape ruling** the open questions leave open — most immediately, **which session
  table clause 1 counts** (`public.job_simulation_sessions` vs `jobsimulation.sessions`) and **what
  "COMPLETES" means per modality**.

**What does NOT belong here:** a re-wording of a gate clause to make it passable. That is the specific way
a checkpoint layer dies, and `overview.md` lists it as out of scope.
