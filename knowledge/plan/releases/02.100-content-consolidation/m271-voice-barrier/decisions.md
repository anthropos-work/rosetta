## Standing context — recorded at scaffold time, not an M271 decision

### M206 no longer exists as a reservation

**M206 ("AI-sim mirror tier") is DISSOLVED.** It was a reservation, never a milestone — no `overview.md`,
re-reserved across five consecutive releases (v2.1 → v2.7) before **v2.8 `M256` re-fated it under
`D-v28-4`** (`knowledge/plan/roadmap-vision.md:312-345`, *"this is their last reservation … Do not
re-reserve them a sixth time"*).

**M271 is where its scope lands.** The **voice (LiveKit)** half is this milestone's subject. M271 also
**inherits the rest**:

- **recording (Chime)** — the second half of M206's signature voice/recording journey;
- **the skill-paths verify-skill end-to-end TERMINAL** — one of the non-gate legs M206 absorbed at the
  M203 close (2026-07-02, Fate-3).

**Consequence for anyone reading a routing that says "→ M206": there is no such destination.** Route to
M271, or to whatever written disposition M271 gives the inherited items.

**Why this is recorded here rather than only in `roadmap-vision.md`:** the M258 lesson — *a routing
written in a closing milestone's decisions is not a routing until the target's own doc says so.* M258
recorded that lesson in its own `overview.md` and the very next close repeated the failure. This block is
the destination saying so.

⚠️ **`playthroughs/manifest/ai-simulations.yaml:4` still points at M206** — *"NON-VOICE only this release;
voice (LiveKit) + recording (Chime) -> the M206 mirror tier"*. That reference is now dangling. Repointing
it is in scope for whatever verdict M271 reaches; it is **not** a licence to edit the manifest before the
verdict exists.
