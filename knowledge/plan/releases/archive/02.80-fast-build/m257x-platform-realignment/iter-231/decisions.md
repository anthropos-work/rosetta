# iter-231 — decisions

## `D-M257x-231-1` — the broad reading is disclosed, not buried

The broad pattern returns **75 sites / 28 shas** and reads as a corpus riddled with stale refs. The strict,
sealed-in-advance population is **6**. Publishing only the flattering number would hide a real judgement
call: the 69-site difference is entirely **dated past-tense** prose, and *whether that form counts* is the
whole question. So the close states both, and states which one the sealed escalation condition selected —
so a later reader can disagree with the partition rather than having to rediscover that one was made.

## `D-M257x-231-2` — a stale ref phrase is repaired by DATING it, never by bumping the sha

`platform-alignment.md:1476` calls `2adcf71` *"Origin HEAD"* in the present tense. `2adcf71` is 6 commits
behind platform's `origin/main`. The reflex — inherited from a hundred anchor repairs in this milestone —
is to update the stale value.

**Here that would be the defect.** The paragraph exists to explain that two of iter-21's corrections were
false *at `2adcf71`*; the sha is the subject of the claim, not a pointer to it. Replacing it with
`0c91421d` would assert something nobody measured, about a ref where the quoted comment may not even exist.

The mirror of `anchor_construct_guard`'s own instruction — *"Repair the CITATION against the subject —
never bump the offset"* — with the operands swapped: **when the ref IS the subject, repair the tense.**
