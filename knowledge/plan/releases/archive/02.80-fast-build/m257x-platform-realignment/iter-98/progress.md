## The discovery-pool question, answered with a measurement

Full working in [`discovery-pool.md`](./discovery-pool.md). The headline:

**Predicate WIDTH is collapsing, and width is a property of the corpus rather than of the reading's recall.**

| | iter-96 | **iter-98** |
|---|---|---|
| mean sites / predicate | 3.64 | **1.76** |
| max predicate width | 11 | **4** |
| predicates of width ≥ 6 | 4 | **0** |
| predicates of width 1 | 4 of 14 (29 %) | **11 of 21 (52 %)** |

Over half of what remains propagates to exactly one site, and the method got *stronger* between the passes
(the paraphrase axis can only increase measured width), so this is not an artifact of measurement.

**The classes split into a mechanizable majority and an open-ended remainder.** Draining and enumerable:
platform-drift (**7 of 13 → 1 of 20 → 0 of 17 predicates** across three readings), wrong-construct citations
(fully enumerable — `anchor_construct_guard` scans the whole anchor set), counts with a derivable source,
stale currency pins. **Not bounded by anything we have built:** scoping errors (no artifact declares a
sentence's intended scope), model-changed-underneath (generated by our *own* rext development), and
intra-document self-contradiction. **Two of iter-98's three widest predicates were open-ended ones.**

**What this does NOT establish — and the reason it may mislead.** It does not establish that `N` will fall.
`N` counts what a ~68 %-union-recall instrument surfaces, two readings on different trees are not a series,
and — the new point — **the composition shift should itself depress recall**: a predicate at 11 sites gives
14 blind seats eleven chances to catch it, one at a single site gives one. Capture–recapture assumes
homogeneous detectability and biases *down* when that fails. So a shrinking pool measured by a degrading
instrument looks exactly like a growing pool. iter-97 already shows the signature (per-pass recall
60/42 % → 41/44 %, union 78 % → 68 %, while the platform class collapsed). **Do not read `N` as the
convergence signal; it remains the gate, un-recut.**
