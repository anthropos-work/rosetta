## `D-M257x-281-1` — `FIX-M257x-278` is closed at the CLASS, and the route's premise is retracted

The route named two files and predicted a loud failure elsewhere. Both are wrong: the population is **six
sites in three files**, and on the authoring box the hardcoded root **still resolves**, so from a clone the
tests reported a full pass while reading the ORIGINAL tree. **Retracted in the fence's docstring rather
than silently corrected** — a route that mis-describes its own failure mode sends the next reader looking
for a crash that never comes.

## `D-M257x-281-2` — the fence's first draft committed the defect it was written to catch

Drafted line-grained, it flagged its own docstring (plus a comment about another host and a fixture
string). **Sixth instance of instrument-inside-its-subject this milestone, and the only one authored by the
fence built for that class.** Kept in the record. The predicate became a parsed construct: a home-root
literal is a finding only where it RESOLVES. **Quotation is not coupling** — and a fence that cannot tell
the two apart would have demanded the corpus stop citing paths on other hosts.

## `D-M257x-281-3` — iter-280's 13-failure residual DID NOT REPRODUCE, and the two halves are stated apart

All four implicated files are green inside a full live run; two candidate causes were probed and refuted
first. **What is established:** it is not a standing test-isolation defect. **What is not:** what it
actually was. Contention is the economical account and **nothing here measured it**. The
instrument-inside-its-subject criticism of `test_suite_census` stands as *structural*, not as a proven
cause. Recorded this way deliberately — iter-280 could not settle attribution and said so; inflating a
plausible mechanism into a finding would be the opposite lesson.
