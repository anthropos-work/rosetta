## `D-M257x-217-1` — the widening is SYMMETRIC BY CONSTRUCTION, and the empty leg is staged

The emphasis run may close on the operand (`**292** modules`) or open on the noun (`292 **modules**`).
Measured before shipping: the close leg is worth **39** matches, the mirror leg **0**. Shipping only the
leg that fires is what iters 209–212 spent four iters undoing — one construct, two spellings, one of
them unwatched. So both legs ship, the mirror leg's live contribution is **declared** as

because a live population of zero cannot prove that its own branch fires (`§9`).

## `D-M257x-217-3` — the underscore-italic OPENING spelling is declined, with its size and its cost

`__292__ modules` matches nothing, because `_` is a word character and the operand's leading lookbehind
`(?<![\w.\-§#])` is what keeps identifier tails (`foo_292 modules`) out of every census. Reaching it
means relaxing that lookbehind. Measured before declining: **the only `_N_`-shaped site in the repo is
the fixture in the arm that declines it.** The closing side (`292__ modules`) IS reached, so the refusal
is one-sided and stated as such. `test_01b` makes the *reason* executable — it removes the lookbehind
and requires BOTH that the declined form then matches AND that `foo_292 modules` matches too, so the
recorded reason cannot name the wrong mechanism.

## `D-M257x-217-4` — the SUPERSET carries the same separator, or the reach metric is a fiction

`_ANY_NOUN_RE` is the population `_MEASURED_NOUNS`'s reach is measured against. Widening only the
selector would have left the superset narrower than the set it bounds — iter-207's *a vocabulary derived
from a population with a hole in it inherits the hole*, one construct down. Both were widened in the same

Four taken as nouns (`refusals`, `sources`, `directories`, `segments`); one declined as a verb
(`crosses` — *"exactly **1** crosses a pruned name"*). Plural shape is not nouniness, for the fifth time.
