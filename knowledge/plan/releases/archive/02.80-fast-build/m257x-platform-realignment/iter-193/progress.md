refs; an unreadable one increments neither counter, so it joined the declared-blind figure **and left
the RECALL denominator**. The published recall therefore **rises when the instrument loses a case**.

again, one iter later, in a different module.

walked past it.

## The repair (`D-M257x-193-3`)

partition assertion that refuses to let a ratio be read when the accounting is ambiguous. Verdict on the
healthy tree unchanged — `RECALL 4/6 … excludes 1 declared-blind`.
