# iter-135 — the twelve seats that never got an independent adjudicator

## Active strategy reference

**No successor strategy is authorable** — `TOK-08`'s sealed refutation branch bars one; running under
the user's direct brief, as iters 121–134 did.

## Step 0 — re-survey

`FIX-M257x-iter131-adjudication-independence` is **the oldest unactioned route on the milestone**.
iter-131 itself called it *"the first item the next iter should action"*, and **three consecutive iters
(132, 133, 134) passed it over** — iter-134's close named that fact rather than letting it lapse
quietly. It is still live: `iter-131/verdicts/` contains exactly **one** file, `adj-1.md`, covering
seats `r33-A` + `r34-A`. The other **twelve** seat reports were adjudicated by the coordinator.

## Cluster / target identified

**The method deviation iter-131 disclosed against itself.** Its own words:

> ⚠ *"the 200-subagent cap left ONE independent adjudicator; I adjudicated the other twelve seats
> myself while being the author of three upheld predicates."*

**And the deviation already cost something measurable, in the direction predicted.** `adj-1` — the one
seat block that got an independent agent — **refuted the coordinator's framing of the largest cluster
within an hour of landing**, merging two predicates into one and moving `P` **30 → 29**. Twelve seats
did not get that scrutiny.

## Hypothesis

If self-adjudication inflated the reading, independent adjudicators over the remaining twelve seats
will **disagree with the coordinator at a rate above `adj-1`'s single data point**, and the
disagreements will concentrate in **predicate framing** (as `adj-1`'s did) rather than in per-anchor
truth.

## Expected lift

**No new reading is taken and `P`/`N` are NOT re-cut by this iter** — re-adjudicating an existing seat
set is not a fresh sample, and treating it as one would manufacture a movement out of a method fix.
What this iter produces is a **corrected verdict sheet** for a reading already published, plus the
disagreement rate itself, which is a measurement of the instrument rather than of the corpus.

## Phase plan

1. Deal the twelve remaining seats to **six independent adjudicators** (one per seat-letter B–G,
   matching `adj-1`'s r33+r34 pairing), each under `iter-131/adjudicator-brief.md` **unmodified**.
2. Hard bars enforced in every prompt: no `knowledge/plan/**` except the brief and the two assigned
   seat reports; no other adjudicator's output; read-only; a seat's citation is not evidence until the
   adjudicator opens it.
3. **One addition to the brief's instructions, and it is necessary**: the corpus has been *repaired*
   since these seats were dealt (iters 132–133). An adjudicator must grade the claim **as booked**
   against the text the seat quoted — `UPHELD (since-repaired)` where the defect was real and is now
   fixed — because rejecting a claim for having been fixed would score the repair as a refutation.
4. Collate; compare each block's verdict against the coordinator's; report the disagreement rate.

## Escalation conditions

- If independent adjudicators overturn a **large** share, the milestone's published `P` values rest on
  self-adjudication and that is a user-facing finding about every reading, not a defect of one iter →
  surface rather than quietly re-cut.

## Acceptable close-no-lift outcomes

- **The coordinator's verdicts are largely upheld.** That is a real result — it bounds the damage from
  the disclosed deviation — and it is the outcome that would let the milestone stop re-litigating it.
