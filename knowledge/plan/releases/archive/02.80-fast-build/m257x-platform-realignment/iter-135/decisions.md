# iter-135 — decisions

## `D-M257x-135-1` — `P` and `N` are NOT re-cut by a re-adjudication, and the temptation to do so is named

Six independent adjudicators produced a **95.0 %** upheld rate against the coordinator's **89.5 %**, and
settled all four of its CANNOT-SETTLE. It would be arithmetically easy — and completely wrong — to
publish a new `P` from that.

**Decision: the reading stands at `P = 29` / `N = 47`, and this iter publishes a corrected VERDICT SHEET
rather than a new measurement.** Re-adjudicating an *existing* seat set is not a fresh sample: the seats
were dealt once, blind, at one corpus ref, under one pre-registration. Changing who graded them improves
the grading; it does not draw a new sample, and a `P` derived from it would be a movement manufactured
out of a method fix.

**Why this needs to be a decision:** clause 5 is met only by a reading that returns zero, and there is
standing pressure — four user rulings' worth — against re-cutting it. **The failure mode here is the
flattering direction**: a milestone that re-grades its own old sample every time it improves the method
can walk `P` anywhere it likes. `D-M257x-122-3`'s ruling — *rates from a reading describe the reading* —
applies to re-gradings of that reading too.

## `D-M257x-135-2` — the added instruction is disclosed, because it could have manufactured the opposite result

The brief was run **unmodified** except for one addition: *the corpus has been repaired since these
seats were dealt; grade the claim as booked and mark `UPHELD (since-repaired)`.*

**Decision: state it as a deviation from `adj-1`'s conditions, not fold it in silently.** `adj-1` ran
before iters 132–133 and needed no such instruction; these six ran after. **Without it, every claim
iters 132–133 correctly repaired would have been REJECTED for no longer being visible — scoring the
repair as a refutation and driving the upheld rate down.** With it, the rate is comparable to `adj-1`'s.
The instruction is therefore load-bearing on the headline number and must ride with it.

**The control that it did not simply inflate the rate:** `adj-C` reported **10 since-repaired and 2
still live**; `adj-F` **5 of 9** since-repaired with three still live; `adj-G` and `adj-E` upheld
predominantly **legacy** defects that no repair had touched. The since-repaired marking is a minority
of the upheld set and is itemised per sheet, so a reader can recompute the rate without it.

**Not decided, deliberately:** whether a future reading should be re-adjudicated as a matter of course.
That is a protocol change and it belongs to a harden pass or a scope decision, not to the iter that
happened to demonstrate the value once.
