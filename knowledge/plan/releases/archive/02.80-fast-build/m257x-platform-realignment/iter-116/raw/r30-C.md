- **Anchor:** `corpus/architecture/ai_architecture.md:92` — *"The row's construction rule is

  So the citation lands five lines short, on a sentence that says nothing about enumerating constants.
  This is §5 rule 46's *"an anchor that resolves but names the wrong construct"* — the anchor is
  **load-bearing**: it is the evidence offered for the claim that the row is built by enumeration and

- **Confidence:** high (that the anchor is wrong) / medium (that it is a BLOCKER rather than a MINOR).
  **Why I hesitated:** §5 rule 25 records that the canonical grading rule carved *line drift* out as
  MINOR. I book it because (a) the instrument I was handed lists *"wrong line anchors that point at the
  wrong construct"* under BLOCKER and resolves doubt upward, and (b) unlike ordinary drift this anchor
  is the sentence's only evidence, so the wrongness is material, not cosmetic.
