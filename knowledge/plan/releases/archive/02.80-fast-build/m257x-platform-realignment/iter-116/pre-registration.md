## What this reading can and cannot separate — say it before the number, not after

iter-109 already closed the ARRIVAL-vs-DETECTION question: **DETECTION** (`D-M257x-109-3`). The pool is
standing, not flowing. This reading does not re-open that. It separates a different pair, and the pair is
the whole of `TOK-07`:

- **DENOMINATOR** — the pool did not drain at iter-109 *because* the repair only ever visited sites a
  prior reading had detected. Enumerate each predicate corpus-wide, close every instance, and the pool
  drains.
- **VOLUME** — the pool did not drain because it is simply much larger than any single reading detects.
  Per-pass recall on this instrument has run **33–83 %**; the 24 predicates iter-109 closed were a
  *sample*, and closing a sample completely still leaves the rest.

**Under DENOMINATOR, `P` should collapse.** Under VOLUME, `P` holds near its series value and the third
flat reading in a row is the answer. That is the whole design, and it is why the primary rule below is cut
where it is — at exactly the threshold `TOK-07` pre-registered as its own refutation.
