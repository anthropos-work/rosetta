## `D-M257x-47-5` — the fences are silent for a nameable reason, and it is not a fence failure

All four fences report 0 sites, correctly. `claim_twin_guard` matches **adjudicated refuted forms**; six
of the seven blockers are **newly written prose with no ledger entry**. `anchor_construct_guard` is silent
on blocker #4 because `external_services.md:489` **resolves and carries content** — it is the *wrong*
construct, exactly the class `D-M257x-45-3` recorded as out of reach when it declined to tune a fence to
the answer key.

**The gap is nameable: the fence family answers *"has a refuted claim come back?"*, and blockers 1–4 ask
*"is this new sentence true?"*.** Those are different questions and the second is the harder one.

**But #5, #6 and #7 do not need the harder question.** Each is a grep for a string that still exists in
the tree beside its own repaired twin. **A leak-check over the repair's own diff — *for every claim this
commit changed, grep the tree for the old form* — would have caught all three**, and is precisely what
auditor G did by hand. That is a mechanical, buildable check, and it is the highest-value thing this
reading found.
