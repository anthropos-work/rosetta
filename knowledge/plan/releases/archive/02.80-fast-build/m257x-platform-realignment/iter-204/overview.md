# iter-204 — the vocabulary the literal censuses see through

**Active strategy reference:** `TOK-08` — *census the mechanical classes; stop sampling them.* Third
consecutive iter on the measurement-literal class, at the layer under both censuses.

**Step 0 — re-survey (mandatory).** `SURVEY-M257x-iter199-the-noun-list-is-a-declared-vocabulary` is
open and now load-bearing for **two** censuses, not one: iter-203's docstring census reuses
`_MEASURED_NOUNS` verbatim. Measured before any change: of **478** number+word occurrences in string
literals across rext non-test modules, the vocabulary matches **106 — 22.2 %**.

**Cluster / target identified.** `_MEASURED_NOUNS` is a hand-maintained tuple **inside the module
written to end hand-maintained tuples**, and everything both censuses report is conditioned on it. A
zero finding under a 29-noun vocabulary and a zero under a complete one are different claims, and
nothing distinguished them.

**Hypothesis.** The list cannot be *derived* — every plural noun in English is a candidate and only a
human can say `4 says` is not a count — so the honest treatment is the one `§5` prescribes for a
declared population: **measure its reach, name what it misses, and fence the residual at zero.** The
sharp question the widening answers: is iter-199's zero an artifact of a narrow lens?

**Expected lift.** Reach measured with its denominator; the addressable residual taken to zero; and the
printed census's zero either **confirmed under a wider vocabulary** or turned into real findings — both
outcomes are worth the iter.

**Phase plan.** A: measure reach over the superset. B: split the uncovered into nouns and verbs, both
written down. C: widen, re-measure both censuses. D: fence the residual + the exclusion + the surviving
zero. E: close.

**Escalation conditions.** If widening surfaces new printed `literal` findings, they are real
hand-written counts and repairing them is Fate-1 in this iter.

**Acceptable close-no-lift outcomes.** If every uncovered word turns out to be a verb, that is a
complete iter: the vocabulary is then *measured* rather than assumed, which is the whole claim the route
disputes.
