  share the same `jobsimulation_id` — the sim IS the position for scoreboard purposes."*

    `20240527131926.sql:7` on `local_jobsimulation_sessions`, indexed at `:20-21`, and re-indexed at
    `20240814085624.sql:2` / `20250508151200.sql:28` / `20250808155129.sql:2` — all on
    `local_jobsimulation_sessions`, the table dropped at `20260729133514.sql:62`. So this is exactly
    the stale-shape residue the file's own RE-GROUNDED banner (`:17-38`) claims to have swept
    (*"Everything below is re-pointed"*).

### B2 — self-contradiction: `service_taxonomy.md:52` says the `cms`/`jobsimulation`/`skillpath` schemas exist locally as "legacy husks"; `hiring.md` says a dev/demo stack has no such schema at all

  schema, `public`, owned by `app`** … the `cms`, `jobsimulation` and `skillpath` schemas are legacy
  husks"*. The bullet is inside **"Tier 1: Core Backend Services … Services (current local
  docker-compose @ platform `2adcf71`)"**, so its scope is the local stack. A "husk" is an empty
  thing that **exists**.
- **Anchor B:** `corpus/services/hiring.md:311-314` — *"**on a dev/demo stack there is no
  `jobsimulation` schema at all** — `platform/repos.yml:17-19` sets `migrations: false`, and app's

  it."* Also `hiring.md:304` — *"There is no `jobsimulation` schema on a dev/demo stack at all."*
- **Which side is right (measured):** Anchor B.

    `stack-demo/app/repos`-side: `app` is the only `migrations: true` entry (`repos.yml:10-13`).
  - `grep -rin "create schema" stack-demo/app/terraform/migrations/` → **exactly one hit**,

  - `stack-demo/platform/postgresql/Dockerfile` is a bare pgvector build of
